Common Questions

What to Expect

How do I know which option is right for me?

If you're not sure, start with a free Discovery Session. It's designed exactly for that moment of "I know something needs to change, I just don't know what." From there, we can decide together whether you'd benefit from single sessions, 1:1 long term or a group package.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, the 1:1 package can be split into monthly payments. Just mention it when you reach out, and we can work out what's best for you.

How are sessions held?

All sessions are held over Zoom. You'll receive a link when you book. Sessions are recorded and sent to you within 24 hours so you can revisit the conversation anytime.

What if it's not the right fit?

I take fit seriously. That's why I offer a free 30-minute call before any commitment. I want to make sure we're aligned. If after the first session something feels off, we'll talk about it and find the best path forward together.
