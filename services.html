The Circle

Group Container — Six Week Experience

Our pain, shame and programming stories tell us that we are the only ones who have been through it. The group container exists to prove that wrong. There is a particular medicine that happens when a small group of people (carefully held, intentionally gathered) sit together. When you hear someone else name what you have only ever thought in private. When you realize that the thing you believed made you fundamentally different, fundamentally broken, is something the person across from you has been carrying too.

Maximum six participants. Small by design, because this work requires safety, not an audience.

This is for you if

  • You want to do this work in the company of others who understand
  • You are drawn to the particular healing that comes from being witnessed by peers, not just a guide
  • You are ready to be honest in a small, safe group setting
  • You want community alongside the inner work

What's included

  • 6 weekly 90-minute group sessions via Zoom
  • Maximum 6 participants
  • Weekly journaling and somatic reflection prompts
  • Private community channel for between-session connection
  • Lifetime access to all session recordings

A Few Things Worth Knowing

How do I know which offering is right for me?

If you are not sure, start with a free 30-minute discovery call. It is designed exactly for that moment of knowing something needs to change without knowing what. We will figure it out together, with no pressure toward any particular path.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. The 1:1 package can be split into three monthly payments. Mention it when you reach out and we will make it work.

How are sessions held?

All sessions are held over Zoom. You will receive a recording within 24 hours so you can revisit the conversation whenever you need to.

What if it doesn't feel like the right fit?

The Discovery Call will give us a good idea if working together feels aligned for both of us. If after our first session something feels off, we will talk about it openly. I would rather have that honest conversation than have you stay in something that isn't serving you.

Is this therapy?

No. I am a guide, not a clinician. I do not diagnose or treat mental health conditions. If you are navigating something that requires clinical support, I will always encourage you toward it. What I offer is accompaniment by someone who has walked through her own experiences of pain, shame and programming and come back with compassion instead of judgment. If you need both, you can absolutely work with a therapist and with me at the same time.
