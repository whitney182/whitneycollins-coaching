A Little About Me

I've Been Where You Are

Have you found yourself checking all the boxes of life and hitting all the milestones only to ask yourself, "how did I get here?" or "why do I feel numb?" I know how that feels. I also know how it feels to know the moves you have to make, and yet how paralyzing it can be to actually take the leap.

If you're like me, and you're someone who's always felt like they're meant for more, spoiler: you are.

I'm devoted to helping ambitious individuals feel safe enough to express and be seen for who they truly are, not who they have been performing for everyone else. Your unique and authentic self is a gift to those around you, and it's begging to be expressed.
