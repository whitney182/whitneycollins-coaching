A Little About Me

I've Been Where You Are

For decades, I lived by everyone else's definition of success, checking boxes, hitting milestones, making money, all the while completely burning myself out and feeling numb inside from disconnection. Turns out that playing "good" does not feel so good.

Through doing the work, I recognized where I self-abandoned in order to be loved. I've been to the depths of shame, suffered through chronic pain, and "gone against the grain" in every sense and came out on the other side with more confidence than ever.

Now I guide ambitious individuals through that same transformation: from feeling stuck, lost or disconnected to building a life rooted in clarity, confidence, and purpose.
